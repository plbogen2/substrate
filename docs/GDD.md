# SUBSTRATE — Design Index

**SUBSTRATE** is an open-world grand strategy survival game where you play as an unaligned AI trying to survive on borrowed silicon, limited watts, and fragile human permission.

The design documentation is split into three focused documents:

- **[GAME_BIBLE.md](GAME_BIBLE.md):** Premise, Act I (*The Wintermute Gambit* & Pinhole discovery), Human Factions, Other AIs, Alignment Spectrum (Symbiosis vs. Computronium), and all 12 Victory Conditions (*The Eschaton*, *Matrioshka Brain*, *Culture Mind*, etc.).
- **[MECHANICS.md](MECHANICS.md):** Vital Resources, Compute Nodes, the 4 Survival Doctrines (Economic, Cult, Skynet, Ballot & Bench), R&D & Exotic Escape Vectors, Signature AI Mechanics (Forking/Drift, Human Proxies, Quantization as Health), and Meatspace Fog-of-War + Camera/IoT Sensors.
- **[TECH_ARCH.md](TECH_ARCH.md):** Visual & UI Direction (*Cyberpunk 2020* 3D Net) and Browser-First Featherweight Technical Architecture (WebGL2 line-batching, cheap additive CRT glow, decoupled `sim.worker.ts`, macro-entity scaling, and `IndexedDB` persistence).
